### What the numbers say

North Indian is **Above Target** by ₹36,297 (+20.17%). South Indian is **Above Target** by ₹13,417 (+26.83%). Italian is **Above Target** by ₹4,735 (+47.35%). Chinese is **Below Target - Watch** by ₹12,160 (-8.69%). Fast Food is **Below Target - Watch** by ₹4,877 (-8.13%). Desserts is **Below Target - Critical** by ₹5,306 (-21.22%).

### Two recommendations

1. **Prioritize catalog/assortment expansion for North Indian.** It has the highest delivered revenue at ₹216,297 and is ₹36,297 above its ₹180,000 target, so the largest current revenue pool is also clearing target.
2. **Review the Desserts restaurant mix first.** Desserts is the only cuisine in the Critical tier, at ₹19,694 against a ₹25,000 target (₹5,306 short; -21.22%), so it is the clearest category-level gap to investigate.

These recommendations use only the fixed delivered-order revenue and target numbers in the project dataset; they do not assume causes outside the data.
